# SheCare OTP + AI Standardization Fixes

This build fixes the signup/login verification flow and standardizes the SheCare AI behavior.

## OTP
- Signup requires a valid email and phone.
- Email OTP is generated by the backend and delivered through SMTP.
- Phone OTP is delivered and checked through Firebase Phone Authentication.
- Both must be verified before the User record is created.
- OTP sessions expire after 10 minutes.
- Incorrect verification attempts are limited.
- Phone numbers accept 10-digit Indian numbers (converted to +91) or E.164 numbers.
- Mobile login uses Firebase Phone Authentication.

## Environment
Configure in `.env`:
- DATABASE_URL
- JWT_SECRET (32+ characters)
- OPENAI_API_KEY
- OPENAI_MODEL=gpt-5.6
- SMTP_HOST/PORT/USER/PASSWORD/FROM
- FIREBASE_API_KEY

Do not commit `.env`.

## Database
After installing dependencies:
1. `npx prisma generate`
2. `npx prisma migrate dev --name otp_verification`

If this is an existing database with important data, inspect the migration plan before applying it.

## AI
The assistant uses the backend OpenAI Responses API. Its behavior is constrained to:
- health education and SheCare navigation
- no diagnosis or prescribing
- no fabricated records
- emergency escalation when appropriate
- consistent concise response structure
- privacy protection
