Supported Mobile Health Platforms & Devices

SheCares utilizes official OS-level health frameworks (Android Health Connect, Apple HealthKit) and certified vendor integrations. Browser sandboxes cannot access arbitrary wrist sensors without authorized mobile bridges.

Is your smartwatch or wearable not listed above?

If your watch brand (such as Fire-Boltt, Noise, boAt, Amazfit, or Titan) does not provide a direct open third-party API, you can still import your verified vitals into SheCares by connecting its companion app to Android Health Connect or Apple Health:

On Android:
  1. Open your watch companion app (e.g., Da Fit, NoiseFit, boAt Crest, Zepp).
  2. Go to Settings → Connected Apps / Data Sharing.
  3. Enable synchronization with Health Connect.
  4. Connect Android Health Connect in SheCares above.
On iPhone:
  1. Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.
  2. Select your watch app and allow writing vitals to Apple Health.
  3. Connect Apple HealthKit in SheCares above.
  4. SheCares will automatically read the official recorded vitals.
