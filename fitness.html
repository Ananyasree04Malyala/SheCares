Warrior II (Virabhadrasana II)
Beginner
Description:
Strengthens thighs, opens hips and chest, enhances focus and balance.
Instructions:
Front knee at 90°, arms outstretched parallel to the floor.
Target Areas:
Hips, Quads, Ankles
Benefits:
Strengthens legs, opens hips and chest.
Alignment Tips: Align front knee at 90° over ankle; gaze over front fingertips.
Breathing: Inhale to lengthen spine; exhale to ground down through feet.
Common Mistakes: Knee past ankle; slouching spine; drooping arms.
Precautions: Avoid if acute knee or hip inflammation is present.
